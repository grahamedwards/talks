Role of (proglacial) lakes in the carbon cycle

  • Rapid sedimentation rates sequester organic carbon from bio-/atmosphere (re)cycling. Sobek+ 2009, Yan+ 2026
  • Probable (minor) methane source to atmosphere during Last Termination. Brosius+ 2024
  • Lacustrine carbon source/sink systematics are complicated, in general. Grasset+ 2025

Conclusions

Transient proglacial lakes may provide millenial-scale storage of carbon from sub- and periglacial sources
  • Buried organic carbon oxidizes, remobilizes after lake drainage.
  • Lowered local water tables… or not? Levy 1998, Wu+ 2021

Future work

  • Quantify carbon sources and degree of remobilization
  • How does this scale across the GLH basin?
